Support

Need help? Get in touch by email.

Developer: 刘祥 (Liu Xiang)

Contact support

support.alittleway@icloud.com

What to include

  • App version and build number.
  • Device model and operating system version.
  • The problem, steps to reproduce it and expected result.
  • A screenshot with private information removed, if helpful.

Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary full private backups.

Where is my data stored?

Content is primarily stored on your device. Use the backup features in Settings to keep independent copies of important content.

iCloud backup is unavailable

Check that you are signed in to your Apple Account, iCloud Drive is enabled and storage is available. You can also export a backup to a file.

How do I restore a backup?

Open Restore Backup in Settings and add or select a backup file. Back up your current data first and read the in-app confirmation carefully.

Can deleted content be recovered?

Check Recently Deleted first. If content was permanently deleted, check your own backups. The developer has no copy of your notes and cannot recover them from a server.
